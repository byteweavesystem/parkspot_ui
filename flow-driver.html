Terms of Service

Effective Date: Oct 24, 2026

ParkSpot Master Service Agreement v5.0

1. Scope & Parking Leases

ParkSpot facilitates guaranteed bay leases between property operators and vehicle drivers. A confirmed reservation grants exclusive ingress rights for the allocated vehicle license plate (HSRP) during the active lease window.

2. 15-Minute Guaranteed Arrival Hold

For Advance Bookings, your designated bay (e.g. B2-14) is locked exclusively. If arrival does not occur within 15 minutes of the start time, the bay hold converts to floating facility admission or cancellation per statutory SLA.

3. Statutory Tariffs & GST Invoicing

All quotes strictly follow SAC 999113 for Supporting Services in Transport: Base Parking Fee + ₹5 Platform Convenience Fee + 18% GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST). Formal tax invoices are issued immediately post-exit.

4. Overstay Settlement & AutoPay

Vehicles exceeding dwell duration without in-app extension are billed at standard rack rates via registered UPI AutoPay or FASTag NETC toll clearance upon barrier exit.

5. Governing Law

Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

Privacy & DPDP Notice

Data Protection Act 2023

ISO/IEC 27001 & DPDP 2023 Certified

1. Data Collected & Purpose

ParkSpot collects vehicle HSRP license plate numbers, FASTag EPC tags, driver contact credentials, and telemetry at gate boom barriers solely for automated ingress clearance and statutory GST invoicing.

2. Plate Hash Tokenization

License plate images captured by ANPR cameras are converted to cryptographic SHA-256 hashes within 60 seconds. Raw imagery is securely purged after 30 days pursuant to municipal smart-city mandates.

3. Data Principal Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you retain full rights to: (a) Access stored vehicle records, (b) Correct inaccuracies, (c) Request erasure of non-statutory dwell history, and (d) Nominate a representative.

4. Grievance Redressal Officer

For inquiries or data erasure requests: dpo@parkspot.in • Officer: S. Ramachandran, Bengaluru, KA 560001.
